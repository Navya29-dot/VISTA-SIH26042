The unglamorous part that wins marks

Why this survives the real device

Judges have seen slick decks fall apart the moment Wi-Fi is switched off. VISTA is built to be believed, not just watched.

No fake "LIVE"
Offline / Synced status is always visible and honest — never implies a cloud connection that isn't there.
Bundled, not fetched
Fonts, audio, worksheets and the Ol Chiki-capable typeface all ship in the sync package. No CDN calls.
Human sign-off, always
Curriculum packs are AI-drafted with IndicTrans/LLM support, then human-approved before they ever reach a child.
Flat where it matters
No blur, no glassmorphism, no continuous animation inside the classroom app — this pitch page is where the flourish lives, not the tablet.
One language, done properly
Santali fully working end-to-end for the prototype. Depth over a 22-language demo that works nowhere.
Free text with fallback
Teacher Hindi goes to the local IndicTrans2 backend for open translation; approved offline packs remain available when the model is unreachable.
